2. Information Collection and Use

Green Mouse does not collect any personal data or personally identifiable information (PII) through Cash Register Rush.

The game functions completely offline on your Android device:

  • No User Account: You are not required to register, create an account, provide an email address, or log in to play.
  • Local Gameplay Data Only: Gameplay progression (earned stars count and number of served customers), selected display language (English, Spanish, Simplified Chinese, French, German, Arabic, or Croatian), and audio preferences (sound effects and background music toggles/volume) are stored strictly locally on your device within the application's private sandbox storage (user://game_settings.cfg).
  • No Server Communication: No gameplay progress, statistics, or hardware details are ever transmitted over the network to any external server or third party.

3. 100% Offline Application

Cash Register Rush operates entirely offline. All game assets, 2D illustrations, multilingual translations, sound effects, and music tracks are packaged directly inside the application installation file. The game does not connect to the internet, does not use external web services, and does not require an active network connection to function.

4. Third-Party Services, Advertising, and In-App Purchases

Cash Register Rush contains no third-party software development kits (SDKs) that collect, track, or share user data:

  • Zero Advertisements: The game is completely ad-free. No advertising frameworks, banners, interstitial ads, or tracking SDKs (such as AdMob or Unity Ads) are embedded in the game.
  • Zero Analytics or Trackers: We do not use Google Analytics, Firebase, Crashlytics, or any other telemetry libraries.
  • Zero In-App Purchases: All in-game cash register transactions (cash and card payments) are purely simulated educational gameplay mechanics. No real money is ever charged or processed.
  • No Third-Party Sharing: Because no data is collected, no data is ever shared with, sold to, or accessible by advertisers, data brokers, or third parties.

5. Device Permissions

Cash Register Rush requires zero (0) sensitive permissions:

  • No INTERNET permission.
  • No ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE permission.
  • No Camera or Microphone access.
  • No Location access (neither precise nor coarse GPS).
  • No Contacts, Phone, or SMS access.
  • No External Storage reading or writing.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA, GDPR-K & Google Play Families Policy)

Cash Register Rush is an educational game designed specifically for children, families, and players of all ages:

  • The game strictly complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the European General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR / GDPR-K), and Google Play's Families Policy (Designed for Families).
  • We do not collect, solicit, or store any personal data or persistent device identifiers (such as Advertising ID / GAID, Android ID, or MAC address) from anyone, including children under the age of 13 (or under 16 where applicable).
  • The game contains no external links, social media integrations, chat features, or real-money purchases, ensuring a completely safe environment for children.
  • Parents and legal guardians can have complete confidence that their children's privacy is fully respected and protected while playing.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

All locally stored game information (stars earned, customers served, language selection, and sound preferences) resides strictly in your device's private sandbox storage:

  • You can clear all local game data at any time via your device settings: Settings > Apps > Cash Register Rush > Storage > Clear Data.
  • Uninstalling the game automatically and permanently deletes all locally stored game files and preferences.

8. Security

Because Cash Register Rush does not collect or transmit information over the internet, your gameplay data cannot be intercepted, leaked, or accessed remotely. Data safety is guaranteed by the Android operating system's application sandbox security model.
